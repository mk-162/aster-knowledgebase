# The members-only live map

When you create a meet-up you can keep it **just for your club**. A
members-only meet-up works like this: everything a spectator would normally
see on a public event — the live map, athletes' positions and trails, the
participant list, the route — is visible only to signed-in, active members of the
club. (A club can also open a meet-up to anyone on Aster; see
[club visibility and sharing](club-visibility-and-sharing.md) for how that
differs.)

A meet-up's page is the **same map-first page every Aster event uses** — the
map is the headline, and About, Chat and the post-ride
[Club Recap](club-rides.md#the-club-recap) open from an **Info** sheet over
it. A members-only meet-up isn't a separate, simpler page design — it's the
identical event page, with the same access check gating the whole thing for
non-members.

## Who can see a members-only meet-up

- **Active club members**, signed in — the full live experience: map, trails,
  participant list, route, and a recap (with a replay) once it's done.
- **The meet-up's organisers** (the creator and club admins) — the same, plus
  the [register and everyone-home view](club-rides.md).
- **Everyone else — nothing.** Someone who isn't a member (or isn't signed
  in) opening a members-only meet-up link sees a "members-only" notice, not
  the ride. It never appears in Discover or any public event list.

There is currently **no guest or family access** to a members-only meet-up —
no share code or invite link lets a non-member watch it. If someone shares a
members-only meet-up's link with you and you're not a member, you'll be
prompted to sign in or ask the club for membership.

**Good to know:** if you want family to follow you on club night, a
members-only meet-up itself can't be opened out to them today. What they
*can* always watch is any [public or unlisted event](../for-spectators/public-unlisted-private-events.md)
you ride or run, or a [Just Ride you share with them](../for-spectators/share-links.md)
— those stay free and login-free for spectators, always.

## How it differs from public and private events

| | Public / unlisted event | Private event | Members-only meet-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appears in Discover | Public: yes; unlisted: no | No | No |
| Who can watch live | Anyone, no login | Requires sign-in and the event's access | Signed-in club members only |
| Typical use | Races, sportives, public meet-ups | Invite-only events | Club runs and rides |

## Your live location and your privacy

Inside a members-only meet-up the normal privacy tools still apply —
hide-my-location and your tracking-visibility settings work exactly as on any
other event. See
[who can see your location](../account-and-privacy/who-can-see-your-location.md).
