# Club visibility and sharing

A club page has a public face and a members-only inside. This article maps
out which is which.

## What anyone can see on a club page

- The club's **name, logo, banner and description**.
- The **member roster**.
- The **Events** view (inside the Rides tab) — public events the club's
  members have taken part in.
- Any **meet-up the club has opened to anyone on Aster**, or another public
  or unlisted event the club hosts (a club open day, say), on its Rides tab.
- The club's **sponsor strip**, if it has one — available to any club (and
  [verified/pro teams](verified-pro-teams.md)).

Anyone can **follow** a club from its page to get its public updates in
their feed.

## What's members-only

- **[Members-only meet-ups](club-rides.md)** — they don't even appear in the
  club page's ride list for non-members, and the meet-up page, live map and
  register are all members-only, and it never appears in Discover or public
  event lists. A meet-up the club opens to anyone on Aster is public instead
  — see [the members-only live map](the-members-only-live-map.md) for the
  difference.
- **[The club board](the-club-board.md)** — all posts. It lives on the
  club's About page, not a separate tab.
- **[The club route library](the-club-route-library.md)**.

## Sharing a meet-up link

Every meet-up card and meet-up page has a share button that copies its
link — the natural way to nudge clubmates: "you in for Saturday?".

- A **member** who opens a members-only meet-up's link lands on it, one tap
  from RSVPing.
- A **non-member** (or someone signed out) sees a members-only notice with a
  prompt to sign in or ask the club to join. The link never exposes the
  meet-up's map, athletes or route to non-members.
- A meet-up the club has opened to **anyone on Aster** works like any public
  event: the link opens straight to it, no sign-in required to watch.

## Sharing an invite link

To bring new people into the club itself (rather than a single meet-up),
admins share an **invite link** from the club page. Anyone who taps it lands
on a join page showing only the club's name, logo and member count. There's
no application to wait on — the invite is the approval. If the club still
has room in its free first three, they join in full immediately; from the
fourth member on, joining in full needs Aster Plus (their free demo
activates automatically if they haven't used it, otherwise they join as a
follower and are prompted to take out Plus). The preview reveals nothing
members-only. See
[invite links](roles-and-managing-members.md#invite-links).

## The club sponsor strip

Any club (and [verified/pro teams](verified-pro-teams.md)) can display a
sponsor on the club page and on meet-up pages — a display-only strip,
typically the local bike shop that puts money behind the club. It's visible to
whoever can see the page it's on.
