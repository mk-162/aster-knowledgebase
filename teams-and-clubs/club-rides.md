# Meet-ups

A meet-up is a ride or run your club gets together for — usually just for
your club's members, though you can open one to anyone on Aster instead. It
lives
on the club page's **Rides** tab (alongside **Events**), members RSVP with
one tap, and a members-only meet-up gets a live map only members can see.

The club page also leads with a **next-meet-up hero** on its home view:
whenever the club has an upcoming meet-up, the soonest one shows first, with
one-tap RSVP, so a member's first screen is the thing that matters most.

## Create a meet-up

Club admins can create meet-ups from the **Rides** tab:

1. Open your club's page and the **Rides** tab, then tap **New meet-up**.
2. Give it a **name** ("Saturday shop ride") and a **date & time**.
3. Optionally:
   - **Repeat weekly** — see below. Works on every club, free of charge.
   - **Route** — attach a route from your
     [club route library](the-club-route-library.md).
   - **Pace groups** — comma-separated group names, e.g. "Fast, Steady,
     Social". These become groups on the meet-up, so the participant list and
     live view can be organised by group.
4. Create it. The meet-up appears on the Rides tab and members can RSVP
   straight away.

When you create a meet-up you choose who it's for: **just your club**
(members-only — it never appears in Discover, and only signed-in members can
open it; see [the members-only live map](the-members-only-live-map.md)) or
**anyone on Aster** (it appears in Meet-ups discovery and anyone can find and
join). Most club meet-ups are members-only; see
[club visibility and sharing](club-visibility-and-sharing.md) for how a public
one looks to non-members.

## Weekly repeating meet-ups

Switch on **Repeat weekly** and pick a day. Aster then schedules the meet-up
for you: the **next week's occurrence appears automatically about seven days
in advance**, so there's always exactly one upcoming occurrence for members to
RSVP to. Times follow your club's local clock, so the 6:30pm chain-gang stays
at 6:30pm across daylight-saving changes.

Weekly meet-ups keep producing on every club — they're free to run, so nothing
ever stops the next occurrence appearing, and nothing is deleted. See
[club membership and Aster Plus](billing-and-your-club-plan.md).

## RSVP

Members RSVP with one tap — **I'm going** from the Rides tab, or **I'm in** on
the meet-up's own page. The RSVP count shows on the meet-up card, and RSVPing
is the same as joining any Aster event: when the meet-up starts, you track it
like any other ride or run. RSVP'd members who choose not to track still
count as attendees. Changed your mind? **Can't make it**, on the meet-up
page, withdraws your RSVP.

When a new meet-up is added, active members get a notification so it
doesn't slip by. Aster also sends **reminders** to members who've RSVP'd as
the meet-up approaches — one about a day before ("Ride tomorrow") and one
about an hour before ("Ride starting soon") — so nobody forgets they're in.

## The register

The RSVP list doubles as the meet-up's **attendance register** — many clubs
need one for their insurance. The meet-up's creator (and club admins) see, on
the meet-up page:

- every RSVP'd participant, and
- whether each of them actually tracked the ride or run,

with an **Export CSV** button that downloads the register as a spreadsheet
file — handy for club records or an insurer.

## "Everyone home?"

Below the register, meet-up organisers get an **Everyone home?** panel — a
glanceable list of where everyone stands:

- **Home** — finished their ride or run.
- **On the road** — tracking and recently updated.
- **Quiet Xm** — tracking, but no update for a while (often just a signal
  dead zone).
- **Not started** / **Not riding** — RSVP'd but never set off, or marked as
  not riding.

**Important:** this panel is informational only. It never ends anyone's
activity, and neither can you from here — each person always ends their own
ride or run themselves. "Quiet" means "no recent update", not "stopped":
athletes in dead zones keep recording on their phone and their trail fills in
when signal returns. See
[offline and no signal](../safety-and-offline/offline-and-no-signal.md).

## Meet-ups are free on every club

Every club gets the full meet-up toolkit for nothing — one-off **and** weekly
repeating meet-ups, RSVP, register, CSV export, everyone-home, pace groups,
routes. None of it is paywalled, and the club never pays a subscription. See
[club membership and Aster Plus](billing-and-your-club-plan.md) for how
members join.
