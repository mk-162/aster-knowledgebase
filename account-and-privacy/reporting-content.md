# Reporting a post, a person or an event

If you see something on Aster that shouldn't be there, spam, harassment, or
anything dangerous, you can flag it for review. Flagging content sends it to
our review team; it doesn't change what you personally see. For that, see
[Blocking someone](blocking-someone.md).

## What you can flag

- A post or story
- A comment
- An account
- An event
- A club
- A shared Just Ride

## Where to find Report

Look for a small flag icon labelled **Report**:

- On a post, story or comment, next to it.
- On someone's profile, under **⋮ More options**.
- On a club's page.
- On an event page, near the bottom.
- On a Just Ride you're watching, alongside the map controls.

You won't see Report on your own posts, comments, profile, event or club,
there's nothing to flag to yourself.

Flagging content needs an account. If you're not signed in, tapping Report
shows a sign-in prompt rather than sending anything, viewing the content
itself never requires an account.

## How to send it

1. Tap **Report**.
2. Choose a reason:

   | Reason | Covers |
   |---|---|
   | Spam | Unwanted promotion, scams, or repetitive posts |
   | Abuse or harassment | Bullying, hate, or targeted attacks |
   | Dangerous | Threats, self-harm, or unsafe behaviour |
   | Inappropriate | Nudity, graphic, or off-limits content |
   | Something else | Anything not covered above |

3. Add an optional note, up to 500 characters, with anything that helps.
4. Tap **Send report**.

What you send is anonymous to the person on the other end. They're never
told who flagged them.

## What happens next

What you send goes into our review team's queue. We'll notify you once
it's been looked at, and tell you the outcome: whether the content was
removed, whether the account was disabled, or whether we reviewed it and
didn't find a breach of our Community Guidelines.

Flagging content doesn't remove anything from your own view straight away.
If you want someone's posts and live location out of your feed and map
immediately, block them as well, or instead, see
[Blocking someone](blocking-someone.md).

## Good to know

- Sending the same flag twice while the first is still being looked at
  doesn't create a second one, it's the same one going through review.
- Flagging and blocking are independent: you can flag a post and block the
  person who wrote it in the same visit, in either order.
