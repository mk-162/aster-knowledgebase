# Blocking someone

Blocking cuts an account off from you: they stop following you and stop
seeing your posts, and you stop seeing theirs. It's for when you want
distance from a specific account, straight away.

> **Important:** blocking is a visibility and follow control, not an
> emergency service. If you're worried about your immediate safety, contact
> the emergency services directly from your phone.

## What happens when you block someone

- Any follow between you and them, in either direction, ends immediately.
- Neither of you can follow the other again while the block stands.
- Their profile won't open for you, and yours won't open for them.
- You stop seeing each other's posts, comments and stories.
- On the live map and leaderboard for an event you're both watching, you
  stop seeing each other's live location.

Blocking doesn't rewrite the past: it doesn't erase ride history, and it
doesn't change a result from an event you've both already finished.

## How to block someone

1. Open their profile.
2. Tap **⋮ More options**.
3. Tap **Block [name]**.
4. Confirm. You'll be told: "They won't be able to follow you or see your
   posts, and you won't see theirs. You can unblock any time from Settings →
   Blocked users."

Once you confirm, it takes effect immediately and you're taken away from
their profile.

## Blocking and unblocking are both idempotent

Blocking someone who's already blocked, or unblocking someone who isn't
currently blocked, both succeed quietly. Nothing changes, and there's no
error. You can't accidentally double up.

## Where to see who you've blocked

Go to **Settings → Blocked users** to see everyone you've blocked, when you
blocked them, and to unblock any of them.

## Unblocking

Tap **Unblock** next to their name in **Settings → Blocked users**, then
confirm. It takes effect immediately: they can follow you and see your
posts again. Nothing from before is restored automatically, if you followed
each other before the block, you'll each need to follow again.

## What blocking doesn't do

- It doesn't tell them they've been blocked. From their side, your content
  and profile simply stop appearing.
- It doesn't take down anything of theirs that's already public elsewhere,
  such as a shared Just Ride link or a public event result.
- It's a signed-in control. It changes what a signed-in account sees. It
  doesn't change what someone browsing a public event or profile
  anonymously, with no account, can see.

## Good to know

- Blocking someone you follow, or who follows you, unfollows in both
  directions the moment you block.
- Blocking and reporting are different actions: blocking is a personal
  filter you control yourself; reporting sends something to our review
  team. You can do either, or both. See
  [Reporting a post, a person or an event](reporting-content.md).
