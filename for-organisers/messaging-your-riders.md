# Messaging your riders

You have three distinct ways to reach the people in your event, each suited
to a different moment.

## 1. The event feed: announcements and updates

Post to the **event feed** for updates that show on the event page, where
registered athletes and event followers see them: schedule notes, course
changes, weather calls, post-event recaps. When you post as the organiser,
your event's followers can be notified, so the feed is your "everyone should
know this" channel that also lives on the page for anyone who arrives later.

Best for: pre-event information, day-of updates, post-event recaps.

## 2. Message riders: a direct push to everyone

**Message riders** sends a push notification (with a title and an optional
link) straight to **all participants** in the event. It's the most direct
way to get a short, important message in front of athletes fast. You send it
from the event card on your [dashboard](race-day-management.md).

Best for: time-sensitive alerts, such as "neutral start delayed 15 minutes"
or "route diverted at checkpoint 3".

## 3. The alert thread: talking to one athlete in trouble

When an athlete raises an **Alert**, you can **message them directly** from
the alert, and they can reply, giving you a two-way thread while you
coordinate help. See [Handling alerts](handling-sos-alerts.md).

Best for: a live safety situation with a specific athlete.

## Which to use

| You want to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Share info on the event page (and notify your followers) | **Event feed** |
| Push a short, urgent message to all athletes now | **Message riders** |
| Talk to one athlete during an alert | **The alert thread** |

## Good to know

- Athletes control their own [notification preferences](../notifications/preferences-and-quiet-hours.md),
  so non-urgent messages may not push to everyone. The in-app feed and
  notification list still capture them.
- Keep the direct **Message riders** push for genuinely time-sensitive
  messages, so athletes keep paying attention to it.
- See the athlete's side in [Notification types](../notifications/notification-types.md).
