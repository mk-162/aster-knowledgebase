# Building and saving routes

Aster has a route builder for planning a ride or run on the map, and **My
routes**, a personal library to keep the ones you want again. Open both from
the account menu.

## Build a route

Open **My routes** from the account menu and tap **Build a route** (or go
straight to it from the empty-library prompt). The builder opens on a map
centred on your location.

1. Pick **Ride** or **Run** with the toggle — it changes how your route
   follows paths and how the route-style questions below are worded.
2. Choose a draw mode:
   - **Auto** — follows roads and paths between the points you tap. This is
     the default.
   - **Manual** — draws a straight line between your points. Use it for
     tracks and paths the map doesn't know.
   - **Trace** — draw along the map with your finger; Aster snaps your line
     to roads and paths.

   Switch between them at any time — the mode applies from your next point
   onward.
3. Tap the map to drop points. **Drag** a point to move it, **tap** a point
   for its options (delete it, or close the loop back to your start), and
   **long-press** the line to add a new point where you press. **Undo** steps
   back through your edits one at a time.
4. Tap **Route style** to set what you're on and how you like your routes.
   A ride asks your bike (**Road bike**, **Gravel bike** or **Mountain
   bike**); a run asks the surface (**Roads & pavements** or **Trails &
   paths**). Both then ask how direct you want the line
   (**Quiet & scenic**, **Balanced** or **Fast & direct**) and how much
   climbing to seek out (**Keep it flat**, **Don't mind** or **Bring the
   climbs**). Your answers are remembered on this device and shape the line
   Auto and Trace draw.
5. Use **Search** to jump the map to a place — it moves the camera only; you
   still tap your own start point.
6. To plan a loop rather than point-by-point: tap the map once for your start,
   then **Plan a loop**. Pick a distance (20–100 km presets, or a custom
   distance from about 5 km up to 200 km) and a direction — north, east,
   south, west, or Surprise me — and tap **Build my loop**. You can try
   another suggestion or keep it. Planning a loop replaces whatever you'd
   drawn so far, so it's only offered while your route is still just a start
   point.
7. Turn on **3D preview** to tilt the map and see the terrain. It's a look,
   not a setting — it never changes the line you've drawn or what gets saved.
8. Tap the stats chip for the **route summary**: distance, climbing and
   descending, and an elevation profile. Turn on **Colour by gradient** to
   shade the route from green to red as it steepens (only available once
   there's elevation data).

> **Auto, Trace, Search and Plan a loop all need a connection.** If you go
> offline mid-build, the app tells you and offers to switch to Manual so you
> can keep drawing straight lines. Manual itself doesn't need a connection,
> but you'll need one to save the finished route to your library.

### Save your route

Tap **Save**, give it a name (or keep the suggested one) and an optional
description, and it's added to **My routes**. If you opened the builder from
an existing route (see below), Save updates that route in place instead of
creating a new one — and if the route is already attached to any of your
upcoming events or meet-ups, you'll be asked whether to update those too or
leave them as they were scheduled.

## Bring in a route another way

Building isn't the only way a route ends up in your library:

- **Save a ride you've already done.** On a finished ride's page, tap **Save
  as route**, choose **My routes** or a club you admin, and Aster turns the
  ride's own track into a route. It's named "Route from my ride" (or run)
  with the date, and re-saving later refreshes it if the ride's track filled
  in further after the fact. See
  [Your results, history and profile](results-history-and-profile.md).
- **Save a copy from a link someone shared with you.** Opening a shared
  route link (see below) offers **Save to my routes** if you're signed in.

Aster doesn't currently offer a way to upload a GPX file straight into your
personal library — that entry point exists for a club's route library. If
you're not a club admin, recreate the route with Auto, Trace or Plan a loop
above, or ask whoever holds the GPX to add it to a club library and share the
link with you instead. See
[The club route library](../teams-and-clubs/the-club-route-library.md).

## Your route library

Every route you own lives in **My routes**, plus the library of any club you
admin. Filter by where a route lives, how it arrived (built, uploaded GPX, or
from an activity), and distance, and sort newest first, longest first or by
name. Each card shows the route's shape, distance, elevation gain and a
source label.

Tap a route to open it: the full map, distance, elevation gain, an elevation
profile where one exists, and an editable description. Its card menu offers:

- **View route** — opens the route page above.
- **Duplicate** — opens the builder on a copy of the line; saving creates a
  new route in your personal library and leaves the original untouched.
- **Use in event or meet-up** — attaches a copy of the route to one of your
  own upcoming events or meet-ups. If you don't organise any, it tells you
  so rather than showing an empty list.
- **Edit route** — opens the builder on the route itself, including a route
  that arrived as a GPX upload or from a saved ride: Aster works out an
  editable line for it automatically.
- **Share route** — mints a public link at `astertrack.app/routes/shared/…`
  that anyone can open with no account: the map, distance, elevation profile
  and a **Download GPX** button. A signed-in visitor also gets **Save to my
  routes** and **Use in event or meet-up**. Turn sharing off again from the
  route's page — the link stops working for everyone it was sent to, though
  anyone who already saved a copy keeps theirs.
- **Delete** — removes it from your library. Any event or meet-up that
  already used it keeps its own copy, since attaching a route copies it.

From a personal route's own page you can also **Save to club library**,
which copies it into a club you admin without touching the personal
original — see [The club route library](../teams-and-clubs/the-club-route-library.md)
for what happens to a route once it's shared into a club.

## Aster Plus and your library

Building a route and saving it to your personal library is free for your
first three. From the fourth, saving a brand new route from the builder —
whether you drew it from scratch or tapped **Duplicate** on one you already
have — offers you **Aster Plus**. Editing an existing route in place doesn't
create a new one, so it's never affected by this limit. It's also specific to
your own personal library: routes you build or save into a club's library
don't count against it and have no limit of their own. See
[Club membership and Aster Plus](../teams-and-clubs/billing-and-your-club-plan.md).

## Troubleshooting

- **Save is offering me Aster Plus.** You already have three routes in your
  personal library. Get Aster Plus, or delete one you don't need and try
  again.
- **Auto, Trace, Search or Plan a loop are greyed out.** You're offline —
  switch to Manual to keep drawing, or reconnect to use them again.
- **I want to upload a GPX file to my own library and can't find how.** That
  isn't available yet for a personal library — see "Bring in a route another
  way" above.
